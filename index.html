Train your brain toward calm and focus.

Neurofeedback is a gentle, non-invasive way to train healthier brainwave patterns with real-time feedback you can see and hear.

What we do

At neuroPATH, we offer neurofeedback brain training sessions for people who want to build better habits of focus, relaxation, and rest.

Comfortable

Sessions are quiet and relaxed. There are no needles or electrical stimulation.

Personalized

Based on your initial brain mapping and your goals, a personalized plan is created for you.

Practical

Most sessions last about 30 to 45 minutes, two or three times a week.

How it works

  1. ConsultationYour first session will include a brain mapping assessment where we place sensors on the scalp to read your brain's natural electrical activity. We'll discuss your goals and answer your questions.
  2. SessionsWhile using similar sensors to mapping, you relax while the system gives you live feedback based on your brainwave activity.
  3. Plan and ReviewA typical plan will take 30 to 40 sessions, but most people begin to see improvements after 10 to 15 sessions. We'll re-map and review every 10 sesions to keep you on track.
